## Hi there 👋

I'm Suhas Guruprasad. I'm currently building large AI software factories. I run thousands of AI agents in parallel to build products (AI multiplexing / AI software gardening). 

Before this, I was 
- Head of AI at Invert (YC W22), where I built the industry's first agentic AI platform for biopharma and fermentation.
- Head of ML Engineering at Zalando, in Berlin, where I founded Europe's first AI platform on Amazon SageMaker and Databricks.

Some of my selected current quests:
- [dasavala.com](https://dasavala.com): a film studio where films are programs. A typed Lisp, an F# compiler, a physics simulation, cloud GPUs. 68,000 lines of F# and 454 tests in under two weeks.
- [ahhhh.app](https://ahhhh.app): a self-driving weekly crossword: hundreds of people play it every week and I do nothing. A constraint solver on Google's OR-Tools CP-SAT builds each grid, AI agents test-solve and edit it, and a fresh crossword is out every Saturday
morning. It may be the world's first self-driving crossword app.
- [quest44.com](https://quest44.com): a deduction game on its own WebGL 2 renderer, under 800 KB.
- [inchwormchess.com](https://inchwormchess.com): a chess coach built on a study of 5.7 million games on Lichess.
- orangensaft: a programming language in Rust in which a typed LLM call is part of the language.
